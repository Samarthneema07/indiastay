India guide

Travel India, city by city

Eight cities, eight completely different reasons to visit — heritage, food, coastline and mountains, all in one country.

🏰 Heritage cities

Jaipur's pink sandstone havelis, Varanasi's ghats at dawn, Udaipur's lake palaces — India's heritage cities reward slow mornings and a hotel close enough to walk to the old town.

🍽️ Food worth flying for

Every city here has a dish it's known for — Indori poha-jalebi, Mumbai's vada pav, Amritsari kulcha. Staying near the old city or main bazaar usually means staying near the best of it.

🏖️ Coast & mountains

Goa's beaches and Manali's mountain air sit at opposite ends of the map and opposite ends of the itinerary — book early in peak season for either.

🚕 Getting around

Most hotels listed here sit within 20 minutes of a major railway station or airport, with app cabs and auto-rickshaws covering the rest in every city.
